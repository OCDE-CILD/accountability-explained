Foster Youth

Explore how Foster Youth students move through California’s accountability system and why counts can look different across reports.

Use the viewer to move through the core ideas in order or jump straight to the topic you need.

Why is this important?

Foster Youth is an important student group in accountability because it can surface differences between local records, state matching, and Dashboard reporting.

  • Students move through multiple systems before they appear in reporting.
  • Different reports answer different questions.
  • Counts can be correct even when they do not match.
❮
Who is included?

Foster Youth does not always mean the same thing in every context. The definition matters because it determines who is included in a given report or system.

  • Some definitions support accountability reporting.
  • Some definitions support educational rights and services.
  • Some students may qualify in one context but not another.
Why do counts differ?

Reports update on different schedules and often use different populations, timeframes, and business rules. That is why two reports can both be correct while showing different numbers.

  • Timing matters.
  • System purpose matters.
  • Report design matters.
How information moves

Foster Youth information travels through a path that can include county systems, matching processes, statewide student data, and accountability reporting.

County systems → State match → CALPADS → Dashboard
