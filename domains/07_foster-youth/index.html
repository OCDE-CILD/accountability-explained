Foster Youth Indicators

Academic Performance

A Foster Youth student contributes to the Academic Indicator only if the student participates in the statewide English Language Arts and Mathematics assessments for Grades 3–8 and 11, meets Dashboard accountability requirements, and remains continuously enrolled from Fall Census Day through testing.

What it measures

The Academic Indicator reports statewide ELA and Math results and uses current-year and prior-year performance to determine the Status and Change values that drive the Dashboard color.

Who is included

Students are included only when they participate in the statewide assessments and meet the Dashboard participation and accountability rules. Foster Youth status is based on any qualifying foster match during the school year.

Why counts may differ

The tested cohort is not the same as every enrolled Foster Youth student. Continuous enrollment, assessment participation, and the timing of foster identification can all change who appears in the Academic indicator.

Remember this

This indicator measures a tested cohort, not a simple enrollment count.

Chronic Absenteeism

A Foster Youth student contributes to the Chronic Absenteeism Indicator only after meeting Dashboard eligibility requirements. Students must be enrolled for at least 31 instructional days before they are included and are identified as chronically absent when they miss 10 percent or more of their enrolled instructional days. Foster Youth status is based on any qualifying foster match during the school year, making cumulative enrollment and attendance reporting essential to understanding Dashboard results.

What it measures

The Chronic Absenteeism Indicator measures the percentage of eligible students who miss 10 percent or more of the instructional days they were enrolled during the school year. Dashboard results are based on cumulative enrollment and attendance data reported through CALPADS.

Who is included

Students are included only after they have been enrolled for at least 31 instructional days. Foster Youth students are placed in the Foster Youth student group if they are identified through a qualifying state or local foster match at any time during the school year at the reporting school or LEA.

Why counts may differ

Dashboard results may differ from local attendance reports because eligibility is based on cumulative enrollment, not simply current enrollment. Students who have not yet reached the 31-day enrollment threshold—or whose Foster Youth identification occurs later in the school year—may not appear in the Dashboard denominator even though they are currently enrolled.

Remember this

A student cannot be chronically absent in the Dashboard until they first become eligible to be counted. Enrollment time matters just as much as attendance.

Suspension Rate

A Foster Youth student contributes to the Suspension Rate Indicator if the student is enrolled for at least one day during the school year and is suspended one or more times. Each student is counted only once regardless of the number of suspensions received. Foster Youth status is based on any qualifying foster match during the school year, making cumulative enrollment and annual identification important when interpreting Dashboard results.

What it measures

The Suspension Rate Indicator measures the percentage of eligible students who were suspended at least once during the school year. Dashboard calculations use cumulative enrollment as the denominator and compare current-year suspension rates to the prior year to determine performance.

Who is included

Students are included if they were enrolled for one or more days during the school year. Foster Youth students are placed in the Foster Youth student group if they are identified through a qualifying state or local foster match at any time during the school year at the reporting school or LEA. Students who receive multiple suspensions are counted only once in the suspension numerator.

Why counts may differ

Dashboard counts often differ from local discipline reports because the indicator is based on cumulative enrollment, not a single enrollment snapshot. Foster Youth identification can also occur at any point during the school year, meaning a student's status may differ depending on the reporting period, enrollment history, and timing of the foster match. Local reports may also count suspension incidents, while the Dashboard counts each suspended student only once.

Remember this

A student with three suspensions counts the same as a student with one suspension—the Suspension Rate Indicator measures students suspended, not the total number of suspension incidents.

Graduation Rate

A Foster Youth student contributes to the Graduation Rate Indicator based on the student's assigned four- or five-year graduation cohort, not simply the year the student graduates. Students remain in the Foster Youth student group if they are identified at any time during their graduation cohort, even if they are no longer identified in their final year. Because Dashboard reporting follows cohort rules rather than current enrollment, Graduation results often differ from local graduation reports.

What it measures

The Graduation Rate Indicator measures the percentage of students who earn a regular high school diploma within their assigned four-year cohort, with eligible fifth-year graduates included in the combined graduation rate. Dashboard performance compares the current combined graduation rate to the prior year's results.

Who is included

Students are included based on their assigned graduation cohort rather than current enrollment. Foster Youth students remain in the Foster Youth student group if they were identified through a qualifying foster match at any time during the four- or five-year cohort, regardless of whether they remain identified at graduation.

Why counts may differ

Dashboard Graduation results often differ from local graduation reports because they are based on graduation cohorts, not the students currently enrolled in a school. Students who transfer, graduate early, graduate in a fifth year, or change Foster Youth status during the cohort may appear differently depending on the purpose and methodology of the report being used.

Remember this

Graduation is not a snapshot—it is a cohort measure. Dashboard follows students throughout their assigned graduation cohort, not just who is enrolled or identified at the time they receive a diploma.

College/Career Indicator

A Foster Youth student contributes to the College/Career Indicator based on the student's assigned four- or five-year graduation cohort, not current enrollment. Students remain in the Foster Youth student group if they are identified at any time during their graduation cohort. Unlike the Graduation Rate Indicator, the College/Career Indicator measures whether graduates demonstrate college or career readiness through one or more State Board of Education-approved measures.

What it measures

The College/Career Indicator measures the percentage of graduates who are considered Prepared for college or career using one or more approved readiness measures, such as coursework, assessments, industry certifications, or other State Board of Education-approved pathways. Dashboard performance compares the current college/career readiness rate to the prior year's results.

Who is included

Students are included based on their assigned four- or five-year graduation cohort. Foster Youth students remain in the Foster Youth student group if they are identified through a qualifying foster match at any time during the graduation cohort, even if they are no longer identified in their graduating year. Only graduates within the cohort are evaluated for college and career readiness.

Why counts may differ

Dashboard College/Career results may differ from local reports because they are based on graduation cohorts, not currently enrolled students. In addition, only graduates who meet one or more approved college or career readiness criteria are counted as Prepared. Differences in cohort assignment, Foster Youth identification over multiple years, and the readiness measures used can all affect reported results.

Remember this

The College/Career Indicator does not ask whether students graduated—it asks whether graduates were prepared. Graduation is the first step; college or career readiness is the measure.

Science

A Foster Youth student contributes to the Science Indicator only if the student participates in the California Science Test (CAST) or the California Alternate Assessment (CAA) for Science administered in grades 5, 8, and once in high school. Foster Youth status is based on any qualifying foster match during the school year using the same annual identification rules applied to the Academic Indicator. Unlike the other Dashboard indicators, Science results are reported for informational purposes only and are not used to determine Dashboard performance colors.

What it measures

The Science Indicator reports statewide science assessment performance for eligible students who participate in the CAST or CAA for Science. Dashboard results display average science achievement but do not assign a Status, Change, or Performance Level for accountability purposes.

Who is included

Students are included if they participate in the statewide science assessment at the tested grade levels and meet the applicable participation requirements. Foster Youth students are placed in the Foster Youth student group if they are identified through a qualifying foster match at any time during the school year at the reporting school or LEA.

Why counts may differ

Science results may differ from local assessment reports because only students participating in the statewide science assessments are included. Foster Youth identification timing, statewide participation requirements, and assessment eligibility can all affect which students appear in the Dashboard Science results. Unlike accountability indicators, Science results are presented for informational purposes and are not used in Dashboard performance calculations.

Remember this

Science appears on the Dashboard, but it does not affect Dashboard accountability. It is included to provide additional information—not to determine performance colors or accountability outcomes.
