Comparing Foster Youth Counts

Differences between Foster Youth reports are common and do not necessarily indicate inaccurate data. Reports are designed for different purposes and may use different student populations, reporting periods, inclusion criteria, or business rules. Before comparing report counts, first understand what each report is designed to measure.

Identify the Source

Foster Focus, CALPADS, Dashboard, and SIS reports are designed for different purposes.

Verify Included Students

Reports may represent current Foster Youth, former Foster Youth, service populations, or accountability populations.

Confirm Reporting Period

Some reports reflect current information while others summarize a reporting period or cohort.

Compare with Confidence

Compare reports only after confirming the reporting system, student population, and reporting period. Once those differences are understood, report variations are often expected—not errors.

State Foster Match

State Foster Match is CALPADS' automatic process for identifying Foster Youth by comparing LEA enrollment information with child welfare case and placement data. Foster Focus uses this information, together with locally maintained student records, to help districts monitor Foster Youth, coordinate services, and maintain educational continuity.

Purpose

Identifies Foster Youth for statewide reporting by matching local enrollment data with child welfare data.

How It Works

CALPADS applies matching rules to LEA SIS and CDSS data to produce Foster Match results.

District Action

Districts reconcile local records, review CALPADS reports, and update Program 190 dates as needed.

Working Together

State Foster Match identifies students for statewide reporting, Foster Focus helps districts monitor and coordinate services locally, and SIS records support reconciliation and follow-up.

Reports 5.7 and 5.9

Reports 5.7 and 5.9 provide complementary views of Foster Youth enrollment. Report 5.7 identifies students currently enrolled as Foster Youth, while Report 5.9 identifies students who were previously identified as Foster Youth but remain enrolled with the LEA. Together, these reports provide a complete picture of current and historical Foster Youth populations.

Current Foster Youth

Report 5.7 shows students currently enrolled with the LEA and actively identified as Foster Youth.

Former Foster Youth

Report 5.9 shows students currently enrolled with the LEA and were previously identified as Foster Youth.

When to Use

To review current Foster Youth eligibility, monitor previous placements, and reconcile Program 190 reporting.

Making Sense of the Data

Neither report tells the complete story. Together they provide a more complete view of Foster Youth enrollment and status changes.

Understanding Report Timing

Foster Youth information moves through multiple local and state systems before appearing in reports. Because those systems update on different schedules, temporary differences are expected. New enrollments, status changes, and reconciliation activities may also delay when updated information becomes visible.

System Refresh

State and local reporting tools refresh on different schedules, so recent changes may not appear everywhere at once.

New or Changed Records

New enrollments, Program 190 updates, and status changes require time to move through reporting processes.

Local Verification

Use local SIS records to verify current status because reconciliation is managed locally, not through CALPADS.

What to Expect

Compare the same reporting period each time, and re-run reports after records have had time to settle. Timing differences are often expected rather than a sign of a data problem.

Reconciling Differences

When report counts differ, begin by comparing the reporting system, student population, reporting period, and Program 190 or enrollment dates. Careful comparison of this information will explain most observed differences before concluding there is a data quality or reporting issue.

Check Enrollment Dates

Recommended Workflow

Verify the student's status, compare CALPADS Reports 5.7 and 5.9 with your SIS Foster Youth report, update Program 190 dates as needed, then investigate any remaining discrepancies.
