Investigation Checklist

When reporting questions arise, the goal is not simply to find an answer. The goal is to understand the process that produced the answer.

  • Which system produced the information?
  • Which report is being reviewed?
  • Which students were eligible for inclusion?
  • Which Foster Youth rules were applied?
  • Which reporting period was used?

Trusted resources for future questions include the Dashboard Technical Guide, Foster or Not?, and the Foster Youth data reconciliation materials.
