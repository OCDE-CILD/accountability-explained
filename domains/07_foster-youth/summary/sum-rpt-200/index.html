Different Questions

A Foster Youth count is only meaningful when you know who it represents. Whether the focus is enrollment, services, or accountability, understanding the student population behind the count provides the context needed for meaningful comparisons.

Who is currently enrolled?

Some reports answer the question, "Who is enrolled today?" These reports focus on students currently attending the LEA and are commonly used for operational reporting and local planning.

Who is being served?

District Foster Youth programs may serve students who qualify under broader education-rights definitions, even when those students do not meet the LCFF Foster Youth definition used for accountability reporting.

Who counts for accountability?

Dashboard, CALPADS, DataQuest, and other accountability reports use the LCFF Foster Youth definition together with report-specific inclusion rules. A student may receive services locally while not appearing in every accountability report.

★
KEY TAKEAWAY

Different Foster Youth reports are built around different student populations. Depending on the report's purpose, it may focus on students currently enrolled, students receiving local services, or students who meet specific state accountability requirements.

Before comparing report counts, first identify which population each report is designed to represent. Understanding that distinction helps explain why valid reports often produce different results.
