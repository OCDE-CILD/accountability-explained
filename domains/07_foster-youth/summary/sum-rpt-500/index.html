Data Sources May Not Always Match

Foster Focus

Securely manages academic, residential, and health records for Foster Youth. Continuous records help ensure critical information follows students across placements and school changes.

CALPADS

CDSS performs weekly matches between child welfare records and CALPADS. These matches identify Foster Youth for state reporting and notify LEAs when eligible students enroll.

SIS

LEAs use their local Student Information System (SIS) to compare enrollment records with CALPADS foster reports. This helps identify eligible students and supports timely local services and interventions.

Dashboard

Dashboard accountability indicators apply their own inclusion rules, which can differ from CALPADS and local SIS reports. Understanding these rules helps explain why student counts and outcomes may not always match.

★
KEY TAKEAWAY

No single report tells the complete Foster Youth story. Each reporting system is designed for a specific purpose and may apply different student populations, reporting schedules, calculation rules, and inclusion criteria to answer different questions.

Rather than asking "Which report is correct?", ask "What question was this report designed to answer?". This shift in perspective is the foundation for accurately interpreting Foster Youth data and reconciling differences between reports.
