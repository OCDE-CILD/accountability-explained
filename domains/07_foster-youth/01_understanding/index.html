Understanding Foster Youth

A high-level introduction designed for newcomers and anyone looking for a refresher.

Slide 1

Welcome

Foster Youth is one of several accountability focus areas explored throughout Accountability Explained.

Although the term Foster Youth may seem straightforward, questions often arise when information appears differently across reports, systems, and accountability indicators.

Different reports answer different questions. Different indicators use different student populations. Different systems serve different purposes.

As a result, Foster Youth counts, reports, and accountability information may not always align with expectations.

This tutorial will help build a foundation for understanding who is included, why counts may differ, and how Foster Youth information is represented throughout California's accountability system.
