Overview

How Foster Youth Appears in Accountability Reporting

Foster Youth information appears across multiple Dashboard indicators.

Different indicators use different populations, timeframes, and business rules. As a result, Foster Youth counts and outcomes may not always appear the same across indicators.

The goal of this section is to help users understand those differences and interpret Dashboard information with confidence.
